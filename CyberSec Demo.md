# **Phishing Attack Simulation Using BlackEye**

## **Objective**

To understand how phishing attacks use fake login pages and social engineering to steal credentials.

## **Attack Flow**

1. **Target Identification**  
    Identify the legitimate service being impersonated.
2. **Fake Login Page**  
    Create a page that resembles the legitimate website’s login page.
3. **Phishing Link**  
    Generate a link that directs the victim to the fraudulent page.
4. **Social Engineering**  
    Send a convincing message, such as a security alert or password-reset request, to encourage the victim to click the link.
5. **Victim Interaction**  
    The victim opens the link and sees the fake login page.
6. **Credential Submission**  
    The victim enters their username and password.
7. **Credential Capture**  
    In a real attack, the submitted credentials can be transmitted to the attacker.
8. **Potential Compromise**  
    Stolen credentials may be used for unauthorized account access or further attacks.

## **Prevention**

- Verify URLs before entering credentials.
- Avoid suspicious links and urgent login requests.
- Enable **MFA**.
- Use password managers.
- Report suspicious messages.
- Conduct regular security-awareness training.

**Note:** Use BlackEye only in an authorized, isolated lab with dummy credentials.
